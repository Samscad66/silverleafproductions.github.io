Carmony

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Carmony helps people who share a car split its costs fairly. To do that, it keeps a shared record of trips and expenses for each car, which the members of that car can see. This policy explains what Carmony collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and Carmony has no advertising and no analytics.

Who we are

Carmony is made by Silver Leaf Productions, based in Nova Scotia, Canada. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Silver Leaf Productions.

Your account

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We store your account identifier, the email address Apple shares with us (if you choose "Hide My Email", Apple gives us a relay address instead), and the display name you choose. Sign-in is handled by Apple and Google Firebase Authentication.

Information you share with the members of your car

When you create or join a car, the following is stored in our cloud database and is visible to the other members of that car:

  • Your display name, your role in the car (for example owner or driver), and your profile photo if you add one (we store a small thumbnail)
  • The car's details: its name, year, make and model if you add them, odometer unit, currency, and the fuel price used to estimate costs
  • Trips: who drove, when the trip started and ended, the distance, and how it was logged. We do not store or share the route you drove.
  • Odometer readings you enter
  • Expenses: the amount, category, date, who paid, and an optional note and fuel volume
  • Settle-ups and payments you record between members (Carmony records them; it never moves money)
  • A history of changes to trips and expenses, showing who made each change and when, so balances can always be explained

Only members of a car can see that car's information.

Location

While a trip is being recorded, Carmony uses your iPhone's GPS to measure the distance you drive. This can continue in the background until the trip ends, and iOS shows a location indicator while it does. The distance is calculated on your phone. The route itself, the individual GPS points, is not stored or sent to us. Location is not used when no trip is being recorded.

Sharing your parking spot is optional and off unless you turn it on. If you do, Carmony records where the car was parked at the end of your trip and shares it with the other members of the car. The app shows it for 24 hours, and the stored location is deleted from our servers when the next trip begins.

Bluetooth

Carmony uses Bluetooth only for inviting someone nearby to join a car. While the invite screen is open, it exchanges the invite code, the car's name, the owner's name and a small profile photo with nearby phones running Carmony. Invites expire automatically.

Camera, photos and receipts

If you scan a receipt, Carmony uses the camera and reads the text on your iPhone to fill in the amount, date and station. Receipt photos stay on your iPhone and are not uploaded to us. If you choose a profile photo from your library, a small thumbnail is stored so the members of your car can see it.

Settings and app configuration

We store your unit preferences (for example kilometres or miles, litres or gallons) with your account. The app also downloads default settings from us, such as typical fuel prices, using Google Firebase Remote Config. To deliver these, Google may process a technical identifier for your copy of the app.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • We never sell or rent your information
  • We never store the routes you drive

Where your information is stored

Carmony stores account and car information with Google Firebase, part of Google Cloud. It may be stored and processed in the United States or other countries where Google operates, which may have different data protection laws from where you live. Information is encrypted in transit, and our database rules restrict each car's information to its members. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy and security information.

Why we use your information

We use your information only to provide Carmony: to keep your car's shared record, calculate who owes what, keep the members of a car in sync, and help you if you contact us. Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the UK), we rely on providing the service you asked for, and on your consent for location, Bluetooth, camera and photo access, which you can withdraw at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing info@silverleafproductions.com from the email address linked to your Apple ID. We will delete your account and personal information within 30 days.

Because a car's ledger is shared, trips and expenses you added to a car may remain in that car's record for its other members so their balances stay correct, but your name and photo will be removed. Leaving a car, or deleting the app, does not by itself delete your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including Canada, the United Kingdom and some US states, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, or to object to certain uses. Contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Purchases

If Carmony offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, they are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Carmony is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or about Carmony can be sent to info@silverleafproductions.com.
