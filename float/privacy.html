Float

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Float is a study timer made by Silver Leaf Productions. The short version: Float does not collect any of your personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Float has no accounts and no sign-up, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, Float keeps a small amount of information locally on your iPhone:

  • Your study sessions, including their length and when they took place
  • The balloons you have collected in My Sky
  • Your study stats, such as focus time, sessions and streaks
  • Your settings, including your override code

This information never leaves your device through Float, and we cannot see it. You can clear your sky at any time from the Stats screen, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, Apple may include Float's data in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Float contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Float Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Float is suitable for students of all ages. Because Float does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. If Float ever begins collecting data, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or about Float can be sent to sfisher@silverleafproductions.com.
